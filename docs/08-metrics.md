# 8. Metrics (plain language, for leaders)

| # | Measure | Goal | Why leaders care |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open KEV items on internet-facing systems | Zero past due | These are the doors attackers are using now |
| 2 | Percent of Tier 1–2 items fixed on time | Trending up | Shows the process works |
| 3 | Average days to fix, by tier | Trending down | Speed shrinks the window of exposure |
| 4 | Active exceptions, and how many are past review | Few, none overdue | Accepted risk stays visible |
| 5 | Share of known assets scanned in the last 30 days | High and rising | You can't fix what you can't see |
| 6 | MFA coverage (email, remote access, admin) | Full coverage | Stops most account takeovers |
| 7 | Critical vendors with a current review and BAA | All | Vendors are a top breach path |

**Format:** one page with green, yellow and red status, a trend arrow for each measure, and two sentences on what changed and what is needed. No formulas.
